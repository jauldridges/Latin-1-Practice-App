Grammar badges

The draft grouping of grammar topics into badges. A badge appears on the map the day its first topic is taught, and its checks ask only approved questions on topics already taught: {{ earn }} to earn it (five right), {{ refresh }} to refresh it (two right). A badge with few approved questions repeats them — approving more in the Review tool fixes that. To regroup, rename or reorder, tell Claude; the list lives in badges.yaml.

Hints. A question's Hint shows its topic's teaching text once you approve it ("hints 3/5" below). Until then it shows the badge lesson's line to remember and a link back to the lesson — so approving the lessons gives every question a real hint straight away.
