Record the vocabulary

Press Record, say the word, press Stop. It saves straight away and plays back so you can hear it; if you don't like it, record it again. Then the next word's Record button is ready — pressing Enter starts and stops, so you can go down the list without the mouse. Silence at either end is trimmed and the volume evened out for you.

Students get a 🔊 button on each word you've recorded. Words without one just have no button.
