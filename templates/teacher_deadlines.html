Rushed cards

{% if saved == 'rushed' %}
saved Every homework count uses this now, past weeks included.
{% endif %}

A card is rushed when a student (1) taps a grade within 1½ seconds of being shown they were wrong — too fast to read the answer; (2) grades Good or Easy on a blank answer, on a typed answer sharing less than half its letters with the right one, or on a wrong multiple-choice pick; or (3) gives a wrong answer faster than the question can be read (1½ s for a word, 2½ s for a grammar question, 1 s per box on drag-and-drop). Right answers are never rushed. Rushed sittings are flagged ⚑ on your dashboard either way.
