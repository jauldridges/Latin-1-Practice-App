Record DI mode

Every sentence, word and model screen a student can hear in DI mode, in the order they meet them. Press Record, read it, press Stop: it saves, plays back, and the next Record button is ready. Enter starts and stops, so you can go down the list without the mouse. Silence at either end is trimmed and the volume evened out.

  • Say the long vowels long: that's what the computer voice can't do (puella vs puellā).
  • A model screen is read whole, English and Latin together, the way you'd say it in class. Read “___” as “blank,” and an ending like “-ārum” as just the ending.
  • Anything you haven't recorded yet uses the computer voice, so you can record a lesson at a time. The placement check and Lessons 0–2 are the ones students meet first.
  • Re-record to replace a take; ✕ deletes one. If you change a sentence in a lesson, it needs a new recording (the old one would say the old sentence).
